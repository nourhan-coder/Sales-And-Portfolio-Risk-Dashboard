# 📊 Sales & Portfolio Credit Risk Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
An executive-level performance and risk analytics dashboard designed to monitor sales revenue, cash inflows, and loan portfolio credit risk from January 2024 to February 2025.

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Findings
- **Total Revenue & Cash Inflow:** Generated **$249.95M** in total revenue with **$110.48M** in cash inflows.
- **Payment Split:** **69.75%** of transactions were installment-based, while **30.25%** were paid in cash.
- **Portfolio Credit Risk:** Total outstanding balance stands at **$69.73M**.
- **Delinquency Metrics:** Overall **Default Rate is 5.08%** and **PAR 30% is 14.77%**.
- **Branch Vulnerability:** **BR-5** exhibits the highest credit risk with a **18.73% PAR 30%** and **6.97% Default Rate**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI / Data Visualization**
- **Data Modeling & DAX**
- **Credit Risk & Financial Portfolio Analysis**
